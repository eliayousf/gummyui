# Gummy UI Product Brief

## Product

Gummy UI is a React design system and premium block library for developers,
indie SaaS founders, and small agencies that want interfaces with a distinctive
visual identity.

## Problem

AI-assisted development has made software faster to produce, but it has also
made a large amount of new software look interchangeable. Familiar defaults,
identical rounded cards, muted gradients, and standard component libraries make
otherwise different products visually blur together.

## Promise

Gummy UI gives developers production-ready interfaces that are fresh, clean,
colourful, and tactile without sacrificing the clarity expected from a real
SaaS application.

## Positioning

**Gummy UI makes vibe-coded products look deliberately designed.**

It is playful and expressive, but not a children's game interface. Gummy
material effects create emphasis and identity while typography, spacing, data
density, and accessibility remain professional.

## Customers

- React developers who are not confident visual designers
- Indie founders building SaaS and AI products
- Small agencies that need a recognisable alternative to generic UI kits
- Designers who want a matching code and design system

## Product model

- Give away the complete reusable foundation under MIT.
- Charge for assembled outcomes: blocks, full sites, dashboards, and the design kit.
- Let visitors preview paid results before they purchase the source.
- Let customers permanently use downloaded code; access to new releases is tied
  to the purchased update period.

## Non-goals

- Copying RetroUI's source, block designs, product names, or paid assets
- Supporting several component engines at launch
- Making every surface translucent or three-dimensional
- Hiding basic usability behind decorative effects
- Launching a large catalogue before its design rules and tests are stable
